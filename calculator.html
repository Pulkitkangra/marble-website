How to Measure

Measure length × width of each room. Add 10% for wastage and cuts. For irregular rooms, split into rectangles and add together.

GST on Stone

Natural stone (marble, granite, sandstone) attracts 18% GST in India. Our prices are exclusive of GST. GST invoice provided with every order.
