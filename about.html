Who We Are

Built on Stone, Built to Last

Ambala Marble & Granite House was founded in 1998 by Shri Ramesh Kumar Sharma — a first-generation entrepreneur with a vision to bring world-class natural stone to North India at fair, transparent prices. What started as a 2,000 sqft stone yard near GT Road, Ambala, has grown into one of the region's largest stone trading and installation companies.

Today, operating from a 25,000 sqft warehouse and showroom complex in Industrial Area Phase-II, Ambala, we stock over 200 varieties of marble, granite, sandstone, quartzite and onyx from 12 countries. Our in-house cutting facility and 40-person installation team allow us to offer a complete end-to-end stone solution for residential, commercial and hospitality projects.

We have supplied and installed stone for luxury villas in Chandigarh, five-star hotels in Ambala, corporate towers in Panchkula and government buildings across Haryana. Our commitment to quality, honesty and after-sales support has earned us 3,200+ repeat clients over 26 years.

1998

The Beginning

Ramesh Kumar Sharma opens a small marble trading yard on GT Road, Ambala with 15 stone varieties and a truck.

2004

First Showroom

First dedicated showroom opens in Ambala City. Stone collection grows to 60 varieties. First 5-star hotel project completed.

2010

Installation Division

In-house installation team of 15 professionals established. Service area expanded to cover all of Haryana and Punjab.

2016

CNC Cutting Facility

State-of-the-art CNC water-jet and gang-saw cutting facility commissioned. Custom fabrication becomes a key revenue line.

2020

Warehouse Expansion

New 25,000 sqft warehouse and display centre inaugurated in Industrial Area Phase-II. Over 200 stone varieties now stocked.

2024

Digital Transformation

Online catalogue and price calculator launched. Serving 18+ cities with 40 staff and 4,800+ completed projects.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🏆

Quality Without Compromise

We stock only Grade A and Grade AA stones. Every slab is inspected for colour consistency, flatness tolerance and finish quality before it enters our warehouse.

🤝

Honest Pricing

No hidden charges, no bait-and-switch tactics. The price we quote is the price you pay. We publish our price list openly so clients can verify market rates independently.

⚡

Reliability & Timeliness

In the construction industry, delays cost money. We commit to delivery timelines and honour them. Over 96% of our orders are delivered on or before promised date.

🌿

Responsible Sourcing

We source from quarries that comply with Indian mining regulations and international environmental standards. We prioritise suppliers with responsible waste management practices.

🎓

Continuous Learning

Our team regularly visits international stone exhibitions in India and abroad. We stay ahead of global stone trends so our clients always have access to the latest collections.

❤️

Community First

As a Ambala-based business, we employ local talent, support local artisans and contribute to local stone craftsmanship traditions that have shaped North Indian architecture.
