Our in-house CNC water-jet cutting facility and Italian diamond gang-saws can process slabs to any dimension required by your project. We handle custom tile sizes, edge profiles, mosaics and intricate inlay patterns.

CNC water-jet cutting for intricate designs, logos and inlays

Edge profiling: Square, Bevelled, Bullnose, Ogee, Pencil Round
Custom tile sizes from 6×6 inches to 48×96 inches
Radius cutting for curved walls, staircases and columns
Tolerance maintained within ±0.5mm across all dimensions

Polishing & Restoration

Years of foot traffic, scratches and staining can dull even the finest marble. Our polishing and restoration team uses diamond abrasive pads and crystallisation techniques to restore stone to its original factory finish — or better.

On-site polishing for installed marble and granite floors
Scratch and etch mark removal from marble surfaces
Crack repair and joint filling with colour-matched epoxy
Stain removal treatment for oil, rust and chemical damage
Honing service to convert polished finish to matte if desired
Protective sealing post-restoration for long-term maintenance

Professional Installation

Our team of 40+ certified installation professionals handles marble and granite laying for residential, commercial and hospitality projects. We use only approved adhesives, levelling compounds and grout to ensure a flawless, long-lasting installation.

Flooring installation using polymer-modified adhesive on prepared substrate
Wall cladding with mechanical fixing and adhesive bonding
Kitchen counter and bathroom vanity installation
Staircase nosing, risers and skirting installation
Outdoor paving with proper slope for water drainage
Post-installation grouting, cleaning and protective sealing
