Why Choose Us

The Ambala Advantage

🏗️

Direct Factory Sourcing

We source directly from quarries in Rajasthan, Italy, Brazil and China — eliminating middlemen to offer you the best prices without compromising quality.

🔬

Quality Certified Stock

Every slab is inspected for consistency, thickness tolerance and finish quality. We maintain only Grade A commercial and Grade AA premium stocks.

✂️

Custom Cutting & Sizing

In-house CNC cutting facility enables precision custom sizes, edge profiles, and radius cuts to match your exact architectural specifications.

🚛

Pan-North India Delivery

Our fleet of specialized transport vehicles delivers safely to Chandigarh, Delhi NCR, Patiala, Karnal and all major cities across North India.

👷

Expert Installation Team

Our trained installation crew of 40+ professionals ensure perfect laying with proper adhesive, levelling and joint-filling techniques.

🤝

After-Sales Support

We stand behind our products with comprehensive after-sales support including polishing, restoration, and maintenance services.
